Other World Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 10, 2026

Short version: Other World has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Everything it stores stays on your phone. Nothing is sent to us. The only thing that ever leaves your phone is a rough location for the optional weather widget, sent to the weather service Open-Meteo.

Who we are

Other World is an Android home-screen launcher made by an independent developer. Contact: vjpisme1234@gmail.com.

What stays on your phone

Other World keeps the following only in its private app storage on your device. It is not uploaded, shared or backed up to any cloud (Android backup is turned off for this app):

  • Your home layout: pages, dock, folders, widgets and settings.
  • Your hidden drawer: phone numbers you type in, notes you write, files you move in, and which apps you chose to hide. This is stored as plain files in the app's private storage, which other apps cannot read.
  • Sounds you record for the app wheel.
  • The last weather reading, so the widget shows something before it refreshes.

Uninstalling Other World, or clearing its data, deletes all of this. When you clear data from Android's settings, Other World first offers to give your hidden numbers, files and notes back to your phone (to Contacts and to your Pictures, Movies, Music or Download folders).

Permissions and special access

Each one is only asked for when you turn on the feature that needs it. You can say no and the rest of the app still works.

Approximate locationOnly for the glass weather widget, if you add it. A rough, city-level location is sent to Open-Meteo to get the local forecast, at most every 30 minutes. It is not stored by us or used for anything else.

MicrophoneOnly while you hold "Hold to record" to make your own wheel sound. The recording is saved on your phone and never uploaded.
Contacts (write)Only when you clear Other World's data, to put the phone numbers from your hidden drawer back into your Contacts. Other World never reads your contacts.
Set wallpaperTo set the wallpapers you choose.
Expand status barSo swiping down on home opens your notifications and quick settings.
See installed appsA launcher needs the list of your apps to show them on the home screen, dock and wheel. The list stays on your phone.

Third parties

The only outside service is Open-Meteo, used by the optional weather widget. It receives your approximate coordinates and your phone's IP address, as with any web request. Other World includes no advertising, analytics or crash-reporting libraries.

Children

Other World is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Your choices

  • Turn off any feature, or revoke its permission in Android Settings, at any time.
  • Remove the weather widget to stop all network use.
  • Delete everything by uninstalling the app or clearing its data.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
